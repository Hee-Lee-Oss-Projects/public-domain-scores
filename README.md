There is an enormous body of music that humanity collectively owns: every composition whose copyright has expired. Yet most of it is, in practice, *unusable as data*. It exists only as images — scanned PDFs of century-old engravings on sites like IMSLP and the Internet Archive. A student cannot slow it down, a teacher cannot transpose it for a different instrument, a blind musician cannot render it to braille, an arranger cannot edit it, and a music-information-retrieval researcher cannot analyse it. The notes are public domain; the *information* is trapped in pixels.
